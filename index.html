Flower Resources & Information

Learn about flower varieties, uses and maintenance.

🌹 Rose

Type: Flowering plant

Uses: Gifts, weddings and decoration.

Care: Provide adequate sunlight and water the soil when needed.

🌻 Sunflower

Type: Annual flowering plant

Uses: Garden decoration and bouquets.

Care: Grow in a sunny location with well-drained soil.

🌼 Marigold

Type: Flowering annual

Uses: Festivals, garlands and decorations.

Care: Give sunlight and avoid waterlogged soil.

🌷 Tulip

Type: Bulb-grown flowering plant

Uses: Gardens and ornamental arrangements.

Care: Use suitable soil and climate conditions for the variety.

💧 Flower Maintenance

Use a clean vase, fresh water and a clean cutting tool. Remove leaves below the waterline and replace the water regularly.

About Our Project

Flower Resources — Flower Shop Management System is a student web development project created using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It demonstrates how an online flower shop can display products, organize categories, manage a shopping cart and calculate customer bills.

The project also provides information about flowers, their uses and basic care instructions. It is designed to make the shopping experience simple, attractive and user-friendly.

Project Modules: Home, Flower Catalogue, Search, Categories, Cart Management, Billing, Flower Resources, About Us and Contact.
